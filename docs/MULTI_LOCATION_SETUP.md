# The Shelter Junior Church Management System

This application supports only the Junior Church children's database.

## Active locations
- The Shelter Anthony
- The Shelter Okota
- The Shelter Community

Super Admins can manage all three locations. Teachers remain restricted to their assigned location and approved access.

The application manages children, guardians, classes, attendance, birthdays, imports, QR first-time registration, staff access and related Junior Church workflows.
